# Changelog

All notable changes to gripstock are documented here.

## 0.2.0

- Customer accounts and credit limits.

## 0.3.0

- Catalog: categories, assets, and kits.

## 0.4.0

- Reservations with availability conflict detection.

## 0.5.0

- Pricing engine with tiered and seasonal rates.

## 0.6.0

- Rental orders generated from reservations.

## 0.7.0

- Invoicing, payments, and late-fee accrual.

## 0.8.0

- Checkout/check-in with condition inspections.

## 0.9.0

- Damage reports and maintenance work orders.

## 1.0.0

- First stable release: full rental lifecycle.

## 1.0.1

- Fix deposit rounding on multi-asset orders.

## 1.1.0

- Webhooks with retry and exponential backoff.

## 1.2.0

- API keys and idempotent order creation.

## 1.2.1

- Fix idempotency replay for mismatched routes.

## 1.3.0

- Audit log and dashboard reporting.
